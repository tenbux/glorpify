Turn any cat photo into a Glorp meme

How to use Glorpify

  1. Upload a cat photo (drag & drop or click to browse).
  2. Drag the three markers onto the left eye, right eye, and top of the head.
  3. Optionally use "Fix Green" to paint over spots the auto-detection missed.
  4. Click "Glorp it!" to render the final image.
  5. Download your Glorp.
